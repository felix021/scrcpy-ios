使用与支持

iPhone、iPad、Apple 芯片 Mac 与 Android 客户端

可以在 Mac 上安装吗？

可以。M1 或更新 Apple 芯片的 Mac（macOS 13.0+）可从 App Store 直接安装同一个 iOS / iPadOS 版，按 iPad App 方式运行。用已购买的同一 Apple 账号查看已购项目即可下载。Intel Mac 不支持这条安装方式。Mac 的完整远控、键鼠和声音兼容性尚未完成实机验收。

中国大陆区 App Store 未上架，请使用其他已上架地区（如美国）的 Apple 账号登录 App Store 购买。Google Play 正式版正在审核，Android 版可先从 GitHub 下载 APK 。

连接前需要准备什么？

被控端必须是你有权操作的 Android。推荐 Android 11+ 的无线调试：先开启开发者选项和无线调试，再选择二维码或配对码方式。二维码由客户端显示，用 Android 扫描；连接端口与配对端口不同。两台设备需要互通，二维码发现需要同一局域网。

配对成功，但连接失败？

确认填写的是无线调试主页面的连接端口，而非临时配对端口。检查 Android 是否仍连接 Wi-Fi、无线调试是否开启，以及 iOS 的本地网络权限。切换 Wi-Fi 或重开无线调试后，连接端口可能改变。

支持 USB 或互联网连接吗？

Android 客户端支持兼容 USB Host 与线材的 USB ADB，首次需要授权；iOS 暂不支持 USB 直连。跨网络可自行使用 Tailscale、EasyTier 等组网软件，让设备之间互通，再填写地址与连接端口。不要把未加密的经典 TCP ADB 暴露到公网。

声音或麦克风没有生效？

播放远端声音和转发本机麦克风是独立开关。麦克风转发默认关闭，开启时需授权录音。远端 Android 版本、厂商与具体 App 的音频策略可能限制采集；受保护内容也可能不允许画面或声音转发。

Support

iPhone, iPad, Apple silicon Mac and Android clients

Can I install it on a Mac?

Yes. Macs with an M1 chip or later and macOS 13.0+ can install the same iOS / iPadOS app directly from the App Store , running as an iPad app. Use the same Apple Account that purchased it to find it in your purchases. This installation option does not support Intel Macs. Full remote-control, keyboard, mouse and audio compatibility has not yet been tested on a physical Mac.

It is not available on the mainland China App Store. To purchase, sign in with an Apple Account set to a supported region, such as the US. The Google Play release is in review; Android users can download the APK on GitHub in the meantime.

Before connecting

Use an Android device you are authorized to control. Android 11+ wireless debugging is recommended: enable Developer options and Wireless debugging, then pair by QR or code. The client displays the QR code; Android scans it. Pairing and connection ports are different. Both devices must be reachable; QR discovery requires the same local network.

Paired, but cannot connect?

Use the connection port on Android’s main Wireless debugging page, not the temporary pairing port. Check Wi-Fi, wireless debugging and iOS Local Network permission. The port may change after switching networks or restarting wireless debugging.

USB and remote networks

The Android client supports USB ADB with a compatible USB Host device and cable. The iOS client does not currently support direct USB. For connections across networks, use your own VPN or software such as Tailscale or EasyTier to make the devices reachable, then enter the address and port. Never expose unencrypted classic TCP ADB directly to the internet.

Audio and microphone

Remote audio playback and local microphone forwarding have separate switches. Microphone forwarding is off by default and requires permission. Android versions, manufacturers and individual app policies can limit capture. Protected content may block video or audio forwarding.

Documentation and feedback

iOS documentation · Android documentation

Report problems in iOS Issues or Android Issues . Include the app and system versions, connection method, reproduction steps and sanitized errors. Do not share private keys, pairing codes, device identifiers or personal network configuration.
