GET CONNECTED

准备好，就连起来。 A few steps. Then you’re in.

01

准备 Android Prepare Android

在 Android 11+ 的开发者选项里开启「无线调试」，确保两台设备网络互通。 Enable Developer options and Wireless debugging on Android 11+. Keep both devices reachable on the network.

02

完成配对 Pair your devices

在 App 选择无线调试配对。用 Android 扫描 App 显示的二维码，或填写 Android 的配对地址与配对码。 In the app, choose wireless pairing. Scan the displayed QR code with Android, or enter Android’s pairing address and code.

03

连接，开始控制 Connect and control

选择自动发现的连接端口，点击「连接设备」。iOS 首次使用时允许本地网络访问。 Select the discovered connection port, tap Connect, then allow local-network access on iOS.

被控 Android 需要开启开发者选项并授权 ADB；二维码发现需在同一局域网。Android 客户端还支持兼容线材的 USB ADB；iOS 暂不支持 USB 直连。两端均支持经典 TCP ADB。 Developer options and ADB authorization are required on Android. QR discovery needs the same local network. Android clients also support USB ADB with a compatible cable; direct USB is unavailable on iOS. Classic TCP ADB is supported on both.
