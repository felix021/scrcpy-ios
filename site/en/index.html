Made for touch

Everything you need. In your hands.

A native interface, built around the scrcpy protocol. Keep your Android nearby, and use the device already in your hands.

Pair once. Connect again.

Pair with a QR code or a six-digit code on Android 11+. Name this device and save multiple connection profiles.

A keyboard that does more.

Six rows, including two compact control rows. Ctrl, Alt, Shift and Win; swipe up for symbols, down for capitals.

Sound, in both directions.

Play Android audio here, or send your microphone there. Two separate switches; microphone forwarding is off by default.

Room for the whole screen.

Portrait or landscape. Use edge gestures, navigation keys and manual rotation, with settings available during a session.

GET CONNECTED

A few steps. Then you’re in.

01

Prepare Android

Enable Developer options and Wireless debugging on Android 11+. Keep both devices reachable on the network.

02

Pair your devices

In the app, choose wireless pairing. Scan the displayed QR code with Android, or enter Android’s pairing address and code.

03

Connect and control

Select the discovered connection port, tap Connect, then allow local-network access on iOS.

Developer options and ADB authorization are required on Android. QR discovery needs the same local network. Android clients also support USB ADB with a compatible cable; direct USB is unavailable on iOS. Classic TCP ADB is supported on both.
