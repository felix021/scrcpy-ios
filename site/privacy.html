隐私政策

Remote · scrcpy（远控 · scrcpy）
更新日期：2026-11-12

我们处理什么信息

App 在你的客户端与授权的 Android 设备之间传输屏幕画面、远端系统声音、触摸、按键和你发送的文字。你主动开启麦克风转发后，本机录音才会发送到当前连接的 Android；默认关闭。App 不会将这些内容发送到我们的服务器，也不会自动保存会话录音或录像。

保存在设备上的信息

连接地址、端口、主机名称、画质与控制选项保存在 App 的本地配置中；ADB 私钥保存在系统 Keychain（iOS）或应用私有存储（Android），不会发送给远端或上传到我们的服务器。ADB 公钥和本机名称用于远端授权，会发送给你连接的设备，并可能保留在它的授权设备列表中。iOS 的连接配置文件不参与 iCloud 备份；语言、本机名称等系统偏好可能按系统设置备份。ADB 私钥仅限本机使用。配对码仅用于当次配对，不作为连接配置保存。

权限与设备连接

本地网络权限用于发现、配对和连接 Android；麦克风权限仅在你开启麦克风转发时请求；Android 的 USB 授权仅用于你选择的 USB ADB 设备。你可以在系统设置中撤销权限。连接到你不信任的 Android 之前，请先确认其身份；它会收到你主动输入的内容和开启转发后的录音。无线调试配对后的连接使用 TLS；传统 TCP ADB 不提供同等的传输加密，应仅用于可信网络。你发送的文字可能保留在远端剪贴板或目标 App 中，保存方式取决于该 App。

收集、共享与第三方服务

App 不要求注册账号，不包含广告、统计分析或跟踪 SDK，也不向开发者回传使用数据。打开 App 内的网站或代码仓库链接时，系统浏览器会连接相应网站，由该网站按自己的隐私政策处理访问数据。本项目网站托管于 GitHub Pages，不使用分析脚本、Cookie 或外部字体；GitHub 作为托管服务可能处理访问 IP、浏览器信息等基础请求数据，详见 GitHub 隐私声明 。网站仅在浏览器本地保存你手动选择的页面语言，不会向我们发送此偏好。App Store 和 Google Play 的购买与下载分别由 Apple 和 Google 提供，我们不会获得你的付款卡信息。

你的选择

你可以删除保存的连接配置、关闭声音或麦克风转发，并在 Android 的无线调试设置中撤销配对或 ADB 授权。卸载 App 会移除其本地配置；iOS Keychain 中的配对密钥可能由系统保留，撤销 Android 上的授权可阻止其再次连接。

联系我们

本应用由北京陌腾网络科技中心（个体工商户）维护。隐私问题可邮件联系 admin@oh-my-term.cn ，也可通过 GitHub Issues 联系项目维护者。Issue 是公开的，请不要附带私钥、配对码、个人网络地址或录音内容。政策更新会在本页注明日期。

Privacy Policy

Remote · scrcpy
Last updated: 2026-11-12

What the app processes

Remote · scrcpy sends screen video, Android audio, touch, keystrokes and text between your client and an Android device you authorize. Local microphone audio is sent to that Android device only after you enable forwarding; it is off by default. The app does not send this content to our servers or automatically save session recordings.

Information stored on your device

Addresses, ports, device names, quality and control preferences are saved locally. ADB private keys are stored in the system Keychain on iOS, or app-private storage on Android. The private key is never transmitted. Your ADB public key and device name are sent to the device you authorize and may remain in its authorized-device list. iOS connection-profile files are excluded from iCloud backup; system preferences such as language and this device’s name may be backed up according to system settings, and its private key is device-only. Pairing codes are used for the current pairing operation and are not saved in connection profiles.

Permissions and connections

Local-network permission enables Android discovery, pairing and connections. Microphone permission is requested when you enable microphone forwarding. Android USB permission is used for the USB ADB device you select. You can revoke permissions in system settings. Connect only to Android devices you trust: they receive your input and any microphone audio you choose to forward. Paired wireless debugging connections use TLS; classic TCP ADB does not provide equivalent transport encryption and should only be used on trusted networks. Text you send may remain in the remote clipboard or target app, whose own behavior determines its retention.

Collection, sharing and third parties

No account is required. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs and sends no usage data to the developer. External links open websites in your browser; those websites handle requests under their own policies. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, with no analytics scripts, cookies or external fonts. GitHub may process basic request data such as IP addresses and browser information; see the GitHub Privacy Statement . This website stores only your manually selected page language in your browser; this preference is not sent to us. Apple and Google handle App Store and Google Play purchases and downloads respectively. We do not receive your payment-card details.

Your choices

Delete saved profiles, turn off audio or microphone forwarding, and revoke pairing or ADB authorization in Android’s debugging settings. Uninstalling the app removes local configuration. iOS may retain Keychain keys; revoking authorization on Android prevents those keys from reconnecting.

Contact

The app is maintained by Beijing Moteng Network Technology Center (Individually Owned Business). For privacy questions, email admin@oh-my-term.cn , or contact the maintainers via GitHub Issues . Issues are public: do not include private keys, pairing codes, personal network addresses or recordings. Updates to this policy will be dated on this page.
